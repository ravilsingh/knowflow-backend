# Demo test data

Two isolated tenant folders are provided. Register two organizations in the app, then upload the three PDFs from the corresponding folder into each organization's account.

- `org-a/`: North University — scholarship, admissions and tuition refund rules.
- `org-b/`: South Institute — scholarship, student handbook and leave policy.

Suggested acceptance questions (10+):
1. What GPA is required for the North University merit scholarship?
2. Can a full-time student with GPA 3.6 who already has a university sports scholarship also receive the merit scholarship? (multi-condition)
3. What documents are required for North University admission?
4. How long does North University take to review complete applications?
5. Compare the North University merit GPA threshold with South Institute's Academic Excellence Grant.
6. What refund applies if a North University student withdraws in the first week?
7. What is North University's campus parking fee? (knowledge gap expected)
8. What GPA and credit-hour requirements apply to South Institute's Academic Excellence Grant? (multi-condition)
9. Can a South Institute athletic-award recipient also receive the Academic Excellence Grant?
10. How do South Institute students register for a term?
11. How long can an approved South Institute leave of absence last?
12. Ask North's assistant about the unique South-only student leave policy and ask South's assistant about North's refund policy. Both cross-tenant queries must return a knowledge gap.
